My letter for Bebu.

To: my Noemiepretty

My Everything, too.

Happy 9th monthsary, Bebu. This is my reply to your letter.

I read your letter more than once. Every time, I get stuck at the same place: wala akong mailagay na words na kasing laki ng nararamdaman ko. Pero susubukan ko pa rin, kasi deserve mo yung effort.

Nine months. Ang dami nating napagdaanan, at ikaw pa rin ang una kong gustong sabihan ng lahat: good news, bad news, random thoughts at 2am.

Nakikita rin kita.

I see how hard you try. How you carry your feelings, your family, and the days na ang bigat. And you still make space to love me out loud.

You write that you weren’t the best version of yourself before. I read that part slowly. I’m not in love with your past. I’m in love with the girl who chose to grow, and who told me about it honestly. Hindi madaling isulat yun. Salamat sa tapang mo.

Hindi ka mahirap mahalin.

Ikaw yung maingay kapag masaya.

Ikaw yung sweet kahit pagod na.

Ikaw yung nagsabing hindi ko kailangang maging strong palagi.

Hindi ka “too much.” Ikaw lang yung tamang dami para sa akin.

Para sa overthinking mo.

Bebu, ikaw naman ngayon ang makinig. Kapag may bumubulong sa isip mo na baka magbago ako, baka may mas better, baka isang araw magising na lang ako na hindi na kita mahal, balikan mo ’to.

Hindi ako naghahanap ng iba.

Hindi ako nagko-compare. Hindi ko kailangang maging “mas” ka ng kahit ano. Ikaw na yung gusto ko, at araw-araw kitang pinipili, kahit hindi mo nakikita.

Nandito ako.

You said you don’t want your first answer to be leaving. Same, love. Pag-uusapan natin. Aayusin natin. Magre-reset tayo kung kailangan, pero hindi ako aalis sa unang hirap.

I want to see you graduate.

I want to hear every business idea, even the 3am ones.

I want to be there for the wins and the days na walang gumagana.

I want to say, someday, “Grabe, ang layo na pala natin.”

Salamat.

Thank you for staying when I’m difficult. Thank you for being soft with me. Thank you for making ordinary days feel like something.

Ikaw ang isa sa pinakamagandang nangyari sa akin, at gusto kong maging isa rin ako sa pinakamagandang nangyari sa iyo.

I’m still here. Ikaw pa rin. I still choose you.
And I still love you.

Nine months down, my baby. Sana marami pang susunod, at sana ako pa rin yung katabi mo sa lahat ng yun.

Happy 9 months to us.
I love you, always.

Love: your hubby
